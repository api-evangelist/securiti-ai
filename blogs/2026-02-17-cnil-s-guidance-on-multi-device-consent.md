Introduction In this technologically advanced world, users switch between electronic devices, from smartphones and personal laptops to TVs and tablets. On every device, they are repeatedly shown cookie banners, which can be frustrating. At the same time, it creates compliance challenges for organizations as it requires an extra effort to keep the consent records updated.

Collecting data is one thing, but having centralized oversight is another. Today, organizations collect data from dozens, hundreds, or even thousands of data sources. That data spans numerous data ecosystems and various on-premises, hybrid, and cloud data infrastructures, with no visibility or documented ownership, thereby heightening the risk of data exposure.

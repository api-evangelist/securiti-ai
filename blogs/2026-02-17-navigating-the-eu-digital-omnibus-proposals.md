Introduction On November 19, 2025, the European Commission published its Digital Omnibus Regulation Proposal and its accompanying Digital Omnibus on AI Regulation Proposal. This comprehensive initiative represents a strategic effort to streamline, simplify, and harmonize the fragmented EU’s digital regulatory landscape. These proposals are aimed to cut administrative costs and enhancing legal certainty.
